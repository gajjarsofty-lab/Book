================================================================
1.  PHOTOGRAPHY — NONE IN THIS EDITION
================================================================

This book contains no photographs, and none were generated,
synthesised or simulated.

The machine that built it sits behind an egress policy that
refuses outbound HTTPS to every source of real, licensed,
print-resolution photography of Surat.  Verified blocked at the
proxy (HTTP 403 on CONNECT), for both shell downloads and the
WebFetch tool:

    commons.wikimedia.org          403
    upload.wikimedia.org           403
    en.wikipedia.org               403
    wikimedia.org / dumps          403
    unsplash.com, images.unsplash  403
    pexels.com, images.pexels.com  403
    pixabay.com                    403
    flickr.com, staticflickr.com   403
    openverse.org, api.openverse   403
    archive.org, web.archive.org   403
    gujarattourism.com             403
    suratmunicipal.gov.in          403

One openly licensed photographic corpus *was* reachable — the
Multimedia Commons / YFCC100M bucket on AWS Open Data
(s3://multimedia-commons, CC-licensed Flickr photographs).  It
was rejected on quality grounds, not licence: every image in
that bucket is resized to 500 px on the long side, which is
about 1.7 inches at 300 dpi.  That is not print material for an
8.5 x 11 book, so it was not used.

Per the brief — "if you cannot find a sufficiently good REAL
image, do not fabricate one; change the layout instead" — the
layout was changed.  The book is set as a small atlas: real
geometry, real typography, real numbers.

To add photography later: drop files into assets/ and place
them with the existing place_map()/canvas helpers in
src/build_book.py.  The page frames are already defined.

2.  MAP DATA
================================================================

All geometry in this book is real OpenStreetMap data.  Nothing
was hand-drawn, traced, invented or stylised into existence.

    Primary licence   Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors,
                      Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0
                      https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

    Distributor       Overture Maps Foundation, open data
                      release 2026-08-19.0
                      s3://overturemaps-us-west-2/release/2026-08-19.0
                      https://overturemaps.org

    Themes used       theme=transportation / type=segment
                      theme=buildings     / type=building
                      theme=base          / type=water
                      theme=base          / type=land_use
                      theme=base          / type=land
                      theme=places        / type=place

Transportation, water, land-use and places carry per-feature
OSM record ids (e.g. "w916108435@14") and the ODbL-1.0 licence
string in their `sources` column.  Building footprints in the
Overture buildings theme are OSM plus openly licensed
machine-derived footprints; both are credited above.

Access method: the Overture parquet files were read directly
from the public S3 bucket over HTTPS range requests
(src/overture.py), using parquet row-group bbox statistics to
fetch only the row groups covering Surat.  DuckDB's httpfs
extension could not be used — extensions.duckdb.org is also
blocked by the egress proxy.

Projection: EPSG:32643 (UTM zone 43N).  Every printed scale
ratio in the book is computed from the projected extent of the
placed map and its drawn width on the page.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2a.  prettymaps
----------------------------------------------------------------

The brief asked for prettymaps (github.com/marceloprates/prettymaps).
prettymaps obtains its geometry through OSMnx, which queries the
Overpass API.  Overpass is blocked here:

    overpass-api.de                403
    overpass.kumi.systems          403
    nominatim.openstreetmap.org    403
    api.openstreetmap.org          403
    planet.osm.org                 403
    download.geofabrik.de          403

With no Overpass endpoint reachable, prettymaps cannot fetch
anything, so it could not be run.  Its *approach* was
reproduced against the same underlying OpenStreetMap data —
clip a radius around a coordinate, separate the layers, fill
building footprints, grade the road hierarchy, keep the ground
white — using GeoPandas and Matplotlib (src/render_maps.py),
and the output is placed in the book as vector PDF.

Required attribution, printed in the book:

    Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors
    Rendered in the prettymaps manner

3.  FACTUAL SOURCES
================================================================

Surat Castle — built 1540-1546 by Khudawand Khan for Sultan
Mahmud Shah III; ~1 acre, 12.2 m corner bastions, 4.1 m curtain
walls; conservation transferred to Surat Municipal Corporation
2015; reopened September 2022.
    https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/SuratFort
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat_Castle

Trading history — English East India Company's first Indian
factory at Surat, 1613; Company headquarters moved to Bombay,
1687; Surat as the Mughal empire's principal western port and a
Hajj embarkation port.
    https://nri.gujarat.gov.in/Home/surathistory
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat_Presidency
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Suratte

Diamond industry — Surat's share of world cutting and polishing
is customarily given by the trade as about 90 %; the book uses
the hedged phrasing "the trade's usual estimate is that nine in
ten".  Surat Diamond Bourse, Khajod: completed 26 July 2023,
inaugurated 17 December 2023, ~4 500 trading offices, nine
linked spines, ~6.4 million sq ft.
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat_Diamond_Bourse
    https://www.suratdiamondbourse.in/
    https://www.naturaldiamonds.com/in/inside-the-world-of-diamonds/surat-the-heart-of-natural-diamnond-cutting-polishing-industry/

Textiles — man-made fibre (polyester, viscose); several hundred
thousand weaving machines, the great majority powerlooms; daily
fabric output quoted by industry bodies in the tens of millions
of metres (published figures range from ~30 to ~60 million
metres a day, which is why the book gives a range rather than a
number).
    https://www.fibre2fashion.com/industry-article/2150/the-textile-industry-of-surat
    https://www.txcindia.gov.in/html/D_%20Surat.pdf
    https://apparelresources.com/business-news/manufacturing/surats-textile-engine-drives-gujarat-growth-ahead-vibrant-gujarat-regional-conference/

Ring Road market names on page 5 are read directly from the
Overture places layer (OSM-sourced) within 750 m of
21.18900 N, 72.84200 E.

Tapi — rises near Multai, Betul district, Madhya Pradesh;
about 724 km to the Gulf of Khambhat; mouth at Dumas, Surat.
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapti_River
    https://indiawris.gov.in/wiki/doku.php?id=tapi

Dumas — rural beach on the Arabian Sea about 21 km south-west
of Surat; dark sand commonly attributed to iron content;
published road distances ~7 km from Surat Airport and ~22 km
from Surat Junction.
    https://gujarattourism.com/south-zone/surat/dumas-beach.html
    https://surat.nic.in/tourist-place/dumas-beach/
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumas_Beach

Population and area — 4 467 797 at the 2011 census; municipal
area 462.149 sq km following the 2020 extension of city limits.
    https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/City/Stml12
    https://www.suratmunicipal.gov.in/TheCity/City/Stml1
    https://www.census2011.co.in/census/city/343-surat.html

Metro — two lines under construction: Red Line, Sarthana-Dream
City; Green Line, Bhesan-Saroli.  The Green Line also appears
in the OSM data rendered on page 6.
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surat_Metro
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Line_(Surat_Metro)

Surti dishes (page 7) — descriptions are of what is in the dish,
not of its origin, as instructed.  Cross-checked against Surat
vendor listings and recipe sources:
    aloopuri  — fried puris with white-pea ragda, potato, sev,
                green and kokum chutney
    locho     — steamed gram-flour batter, deliberately soft,
                served with butter, sev, onion, chutney
    khavsa    — Surat's version of Burmese khow suey: noodles
                in coconut-milk gravy with papdi, garlic, lime
    cheese angoori — grape-sized cheese balls in a creamy
                onion-tomato gravy
    https://surtiflavour.com/collections/street-food
    https://www.nehascookbook.com/aloo-puri-recipe-surat-famous-aloo-puri-indian-street-food/

The counts at the foot of page 7 (locho 21, aloopuri 11,
khavsa/khausa 2, cheese angoori 1) are business names in the
Overture places layer for Surat that contain the dish name.
They are a property of the map data, and are labelled as such.

Obtained from the Google Fonts API (fonts.googleapis.com ->

coordinate and distance therefore uses Inter.

6.  SOURCE DATA FILES
================================================================

data/bangle_{roads,buildings,water,landuse}.gpkg
    The BANGLE tile, committed to this repository as the
    evidence behind page 6.  1300 m bbox around
    21.1990335 N, 72.7901332 E.

The remaining data/*.gpkg layers (castle, bourse, textilemkt,
dumas, city, coast, places) are large and are not committed.
Regenerate them with:

    python3 src/fetch_map_data.py places
    python3 src/fetch_map_data.py city
    python3 src/fetch_map_data.py coast
    python3 src/fetch_map_data.py tile castle     21.19619 72.81776 900  roads,buildings,water,landuse
    python3 src/fetch_map_data.py tile bourse     21.11221 72.79618 1100 roads,buildings,water
    python3 src/fetch_map_data.py tile textilemkt 21.18900 72.84200 900  roads,buildings,water
    python3 src/fetch_map_data.py tile dumas      21.07903 72.71520 2500 roads,buildings,water,land

then

    python3 src/render_maps.py
    python3 src/build_book.py
